HIV in Fulton County: where we stand against the EHE goals

Modeled on Mecklenburg County's CHAMPS site: one page per plan goal (diagnose, treat, prevent, respond), each answering a plain question with the county's own numbers. Everything below is real, published Fulton County data; a live version would add FCBOH program data and refresh automatically.
How many people are newly diagnosed with HIV each year?
The EHE goal is a 75% reduction in new infections by 2025 and 90% by 2030 from the 2017 baseline. Fulton's diagnoses have fallen, but not at that pace.

How quickly do people start care, and how many reach viral suppression?
People with HIV who are virally suppressed stay healthy and cannot transmit HIV sexually. Linkage within one month and suppression are the two treatment indicators in the national plan.

What would it take to close the gap, and how would FCBOH track it?
A public page like this one is a product of governed data: agreed definitions, standing feeds, and a review step before publication. The plan below is illustrative.
1. Agree the indicator setThe six AHEAD indicators plus FCBOH program measures (Rapid Entry within 7 days, PrEP starts, retention) with written definitions and owners.
2. Secure the feedsData-sharing agreement with Georgia DPH for Fulton line-level SendSS data; a standing extract from FCBOH's clinical system; AIDSVu and AHEAD by API.
3. Publish on a cadenceQuarterly public refresh with a review by epidemiology and the data governance council; monthly internal view for program managers.
4. Connect to actionFulton County's Department for HIV Elimination (Ryan White Part A, EHE) and FCBOH clinics use the same numbers to target Rapid Entry, PrEP navigation and re-engagement.

What this page cannot yet show

Retention in care and a current care continuum (county-level data not public since 2019); outcomes by neighborhood; FCBOH program performance such as Rapid Entry timeliness; and PrEP coverage after mid-2023. Each is available to FCBOH through its own systems or a DPH agreement, which is why the governance and data-access work comes first.
