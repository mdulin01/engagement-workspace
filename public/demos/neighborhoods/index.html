How this would be used

1

Community Health Assessment

Indicators refresh annually from CDC PLACES and state sources instead of a one-time contracted report.

2

CHIP monitoring

Track priority neighborhoods against targets, with the same tract definitions year over year.

3

Service planning

Overlay FCBOH health center locations and catchments on need, as shown by the markers on the map.
