Why these three

They cover the three things leadership asked about first: running the clinics, the HIV priority, and the Community Health Assessment that feeds the CHIP and accreditation. Two of the three run on public data today. The clinical dashboard is the one that needs what the engagement is designed to deliver:

  1. Agreed measure definitions and owners (the governance framework, Deliverable 5).
  2. A standing, computable extract from the clinical system instead of per-report purchases (Deliverable 2).
  3. A place to join clinical, state and community data (the target architecture, Deliverable 5).
